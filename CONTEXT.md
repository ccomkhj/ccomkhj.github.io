# German Learning Notes

Vocabulary captured while reading German articles and watching German videos, published under `/learn/german` for later review on a phone. Separate from the blog's essays and posts.

## Language

**Session**:
One page of vocabulary captured from exactly one Source. The unit that is written, generated, and reviewed.
_Avoid_: Post, note, lesson, article

**Source**:
The German article or video a Session was captured from.
_Avoid_: Reference, origin, material

**Entry**:
One word in a Session together with its meaning, the sentence it was seen in, and generated example sentences.
_Avoid_: Row, card, item, vocab

**Word**:
The German headword of an Entry, including article and plural for nouns and principal parts for irregular verbs.
_Avoid_: Term, vocabulary, lemma

**Meaning**:
A one-line English gloss of a Word.
_Avoid_: Translation, definition

**Seen sentence**:
The sentence in which the Word was encountered, as typed by the author and silently corrected for grammar during generation.
_Avoid_: Original, context, quote

**Example**:
A generated sentence illustrating a Word. Each Entry carries two: one everyday, one formal or written.
_Avoid_: Sample, usage

**Note**:
An optional one-line remark on an Entry (false friend, governed preposition, register, or a Korean anchor when English is a poor one).
_Avoid_: Comment, tip, hint

**Glossary**:
The merged A–Z index of every Word across all Sessions, each linking back to the Session(s) it appears in; tapping a Word reveals its Meaning.
_Avoid_: Dictionary, index, word list

**Done**:
The state of an Entry the learner has marked as learned, recorded as a date in `done:`. A done Entry stays on its Session page (muted) and in the Glossary (ticked) but is left out of the Prompt.
_Avoid_: Complete, learned, mastered, archived

**Prompt**:
The plain-text voice-chat brief at `/learn/german/prompt` (and `prompt.txt`) that a conversation partner fetches and follows: the most recent open Words with Meaning and Seen sentence, conversation rules, and the closing `MASTERED:` line format.
_Avoid_: Brief, script, lesson plan

**Quiz mode**:
The default reading mode of a Session in which Meaning and Examples are hidden behind a tap for active recall.
_Avoid_: Flashcard mode, test, review mode
