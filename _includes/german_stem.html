{%- comment -%} Outputs the stem of a Word: the token a learner would look up and the one the Session page highlights inside the Seen sentence. Single source of truth for both the Glossary sort key and the highlight. "der Antrag, -¨e" → Antrag "die Eltern (Pl.)" → Eltern "einreichen (reicht ein, reichte ein, hat …)" → einreichen "sich (Dat.) etw. leisten" → leisten Params: include.word {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign stem = include.word | default: "" | strip -%} {%- assign article = stem | split: " " | first | downcase -%} {%- if article == "der" or article == "die" or article == "das" -%} {%- assign stem = stem | slice: 4, stem.size | strip -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign stem = stem | split: "," | first | remove: "(Akk.)" | remove: "(Dat.)" | split: " (" | first | split: "/" | first | strip | split: " " | last | default: "" -%} {{- stem -}}
