Based on *The Pragmatic Programmer*; quotes are its tips.

## 1

Every piece of knowledge should have one authoritative representation.

## 2

Keep components independent so changing one doesn't unnecessarily affect others. "Eliminate effects between unrelated things."

## 3

Avoid design decisions that are difficult to undo. "There are no final decisions": requirements, vendors, and architectures change. "Forgo following fads."

## 4

Build a small, working end-to-end implementation to validate your direction early. "Use tracer bullets to find the target."

## 5

Fix small problems before they normalize poor software quality. "Don't live with broken windows."

## 6

Define explicit expectations, guarantees, and invariants for software components.

## 7

Balance quality, time, and actual user needs rather than pursuing perfection. "Make quality a requirements issue."
