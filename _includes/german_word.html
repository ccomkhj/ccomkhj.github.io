{%- comment -%} Renders a headword for display: the lemma carries the weight, the grammatical detail after it (plural, principal parts, case markers) is muted, and a noun's article is colour-coded by gender. "der Ausflug, die Ausflüge" → [der] Ausflug · , die Ausflüge "entfliehen (entflieht, …)" → entfliehen · (entflieht, …) "etw. (Dat.) ausgesetzt" → etw. (Dat.) ausgesetzt (case markers stay in the lemma) Params: include.word {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign w = include.word | default: "" | strip -%} {%- assign article = w | slice: 0, 3 | downcase -%} {%- if article == "der" or article == "die" or article == "das" -%} {%- assign rest = w | slice: 4, w.size | strip -%} {{ w | slice: 0, 3 }} {%- else -%} {%- assign rest = w -%} {%- endif -%} {%- comment -%} mask case markers so "etw. (Dat.) ausgesetzt" doesn't split at "(Dat.)" {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign masked = rest | replace: " (Akk./Dat.)", "@AKKDAT@" | replace: " (Akk.)", "@AKK@" | replace: " (Dat.)", "@DAT@" -%} {%- assign lemma = masked | split: "," | first | split: " (" | first | strip | replace: "@AKKDAT@", " (Akk./Dat.)" | replace: "@AKK@", " (Akk.)" | replace: "@DAT@", " (Dat.)" -%} {%- assign detail = rest | remove_first: lemma | strip -%} {{ lemma | escape }} {%- comment -%} a comma binds tight to the lemma; a parenthesis takes a space {%- endcomment -%} {%- if detail != "" -%} {%- assign lead = detail | slice: 0, 1 -%} {%- unless lead == "," %} {% endunless -%} {{ detail | escape }} {%- endif -%}
